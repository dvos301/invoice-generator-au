What is an invoice template for Australia?

An Australian invoice template is a ready-made invoice layout with a place for everything Australian clients and the ATO expect: your name and ABN, an invoice number, the dates, the work itemised with prices, the total, and how to pay you. Fill in the one above and download a PDF, or download a Word or Excel copy below. Which version you need depends on one thing: whether you are registered for GST.

Which Australian invoice template do you need?

What to put on an Australian invoice

Every Australian invoice should show who you are, who you're billing, what you supplied, how much it costs and how to pay. A tax invoice must also meet the ATO's minimum list; the template above has a field for each.

  • The word “Invoice”, or “Tax Invoice” if you are registered for GST
  • Your business or trading name, ABN, address and contact details
  • The client's name, plus their ABN on a tax invoice of $1,000 or more
  • A unique invoice number, the issue date and the due date
  • Each item or service with quantity, price and line amount
  • The subtotal, any 10% GST, and the total due
  • Payment details: account name, BSB and account number, or PayID
  • Your payment terms, such as 7 or 14 days

For the rule behind each item, see tax invoice requirements in Australia. For numbering, see how to number invoices, and for wording your bank details safely, see bank details on an invoice.

Invoice template FAQs

Is there an official ATO invoice template?

No. The ATO sets the information a tax invoice must contain but does not issue a fillable template. Any layout works, including this one, as long as it shows the required details.

Can I use this invoice template if I am not registered for GST?

Yes. The online template starts as a plain Invoice with GST at 0%, and the no-GST Word and Excel downloads are set up the same way. Do not call it a tax invoice or add GST until you are registered.

Do I need an ABN on my invoice?

If you invoice a business, in practice yes. Without an ABN the business generally has to withhold 47% of the payment. GST-registered businesses must show their ABN on every tax invoice. More in do I need an ABN to invoice?

Which is better, the Word or the Excel template?

Excel, if you want the line totals, GST and total calculated for you. Word is easier to restyle but you work out the numbers yourself. The online version calculates everything and saves a PDF in one click.

Is the invoice template free?

Yes. The online template and all downloads are free, with no signup and no watermark.
