What's on a good Australian invoice template

  • The word "Invoice" (or "Tax Invoice" if you're registered for GST)
  • Your business name, ABN, address and contact details, and a logo
  • The client's name and details
  • A unique invoice number and the issue and due dates
  • An itemised list — description, quantity, unit price and amount
  • Subtotal, any 10% GST, and the total due
  • Payment details — BSB, account number and PayID

For the full compliance rules, see our guide to tax invoice requirements in Australia.

Word, Excel, PDF or online?
