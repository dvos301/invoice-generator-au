What is a tax invoice template?

A tax invoice template is an invoice layout that already has every field the ATO requires from a GST-registered business: the words “Tax Invoice”, your identity and ABN, the date, what you sold with quantity and price, the GST, and the extent each item is taxable. Your GST-registered customers need a valid tax invoice to claim the GST back, so a missing field can hold up your payment. The template above checks each field as you type.

The 7 things the ATO requires on a tax invoice

For a taxable sale under $1,000, a tax invoice must show these seven details. The template has a field for each.

#RequirementWhat to write
1It is intended to be a tax invoiceThe words “Tax Invoice”, usually as the title
2The seller's identityYour business or trading name
3The seller's ABNYour 11-digit ABN
4The date of issueThe date you create the invoice
5What was soldA brief description of each item, with quantity (if applicable) and price
6The GST amountA separate GST line, or “Total price includes GST” if GST is exactly one-eleventh of the total
7The extent each sale is taxableMark GST-free items when an invoice mixes taxable and GST-free sales

For sales of $1,000 or more, the tax invoice must also show the buyer's identity or ABN. The checklist in the template turns this on once your total reaches $1,000. Each rule is explained in our tax invoice requirements guide, and you can check them at the source on the ATO's tax invoices page.

Is there an official ATO tax invoice template?

No. The ATO sets out what a tax invoice must contain but does not issue a fillable template, and it doesn't require any particular layout. A tax invoice made in Word, Excel, accounting software or the template above is equally valid if it shows the required details. So a “template from the ATO” really means a template that meets the ATO's list, which this one is built to do.

When do you have to issue a tax invoice?

If you are registered for GST and a customer asks for a tax invoice, you must give them one within 28 days. You don't have to provide one for a sale of $82.50 or less including GST, though you still need your own record of the sale. If you are not registered for GST, you can't issue a tax invoice at all.

You must register for GST within 21 days of your GST turnover reaching $75,000, and taxi and ride-sourcing drivers must register whatever their turnover. Switch your invoices to “Tax Invoice” from the date your registration takes effect. See how the $75,000 threshold works.

GST-inclusive or GST-exclusive pricing

If your prices are before GST, the template adds 10%: a $500 job becomes $550 with $50 GST. If your prices already include GST, tick Prices include GST: a $550 price shows $50 GST, one-eleventh of the total. The Excel version works the first way: enter prices before GST and it adds 10%.

Common tax invoice mistakes

  • Calling it a tax invoice when you aren't registered for GST. Use “Invoice” until your registration is in place.
  • Leaving off the ABN, or typing it wrong. The template checks it is a valid 11-digit ABN.
  • No buyer details on a sale of $1,000 or more. Add the client's name or ABN.
  • Charging GST on GST-free items in a mixed invoice. Set those lines apart and show what is taxable.
  • Reusing or skipping invoice numbers. Keep one sequence; see how to number invoices.

Tax invoice template FAQs

Is there an official ATO tax invoice template?

No. The ATO lists the information a tax invoice must show but does not provide a fillable template. Any layout, including this one, is valid if it shows every required detail.

What makes an invoice a tax invoice?

It is issued by a GST-registered business for a taxable sale, is headed “Tax Invoice”, and shows the seller's identity and ABN, the date, the items with quantity and price, the GST, and the extent each sale is taxable. Sales of $1,000 or more also need the buyer's identity or ABN.

Do I need to show my customer's ABN?

Only on tax invoices of $1,000 or more, where the buyer's identity or ABN is required. The online template flags this once the total reaches $1,000.

Do I have to issue a tax invoice for every sale?

You must provide one within 28 days if a customer asks for it, unless the sale is $82.50 or less including GST. Many businesses issue one for every taxable sale anyway.

How do I show the GST amount?

Either as a separate GST line, or with the statement “Total price includes GST” when the GST is exactly one-eleventh of the total. The template shows a separate GST line.
