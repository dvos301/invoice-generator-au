What to put on a gardener invoice

Common lines on a gardener or landscaper's invoice include:

  • Labour — hours × rate
  • Plants, soil, mulch and materials
  • Green waste removal / tip fees
  • Equipment or machinery hire
  • Regular maintenance visit
  • Deposit for a landscaping project

Plus the essentials every Australian invoice needs: your business name and ABN, the client's details, a unique invoice number and dates, the total (with 10% GST if you're registered), and your payment details. See how to write an invoice and the tax invoice requirements.

A tip for gardeners

For regular maintenance clients, issue the same invoice each visit — the tool auto-increments the number. On bigger landscaping jobs, take a deposit for materials and record it against the final invoice.

GST and your ABN

You must register for GST once your turnover hits $75,000 a year — then you charge 10% GST and issue a Tax Invoice. Under that, GST is optional and you issue a plain invoice. Either way, always quote your ABN. More: the $75,000 GST threshold and do I need an ABN to invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Do gardeners need an ABN to invoice?

If you're running a gardening or landscaping business, yes — quote your ABN to avoid 47% withholding by business clients.

Should I charge GST as a landscaper?

Only if registered for GST, which is mandatory once turnover reaches $75,000 a year.
