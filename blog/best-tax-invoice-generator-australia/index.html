What to look for in a tax invoice generator

  • A "Tax invoice" heading. The ATO's first requirement, and the one generic international tools miss.
  • Seller ABN field. Your 11-digit ABN must appear; a notes box is not a field.
  • Buyer identity or ABN. Mandatory once a sale reaches $1,000. A client-name field is enough; a client-ABN field is better.
  • GST shown correctly. A separate GST line or "Total includes GST", calculated at one-eleventh of an inclusive price.
  • Line items with quantity and price. A description alone is not compliant.
  • Payment terms and bank details. Not an ATO rule, but they get you paid.

Which one should you choose?

  • If you send fewer than 5 invoices a month and want nothing stored anywhere: the free tax invoice generator.
  • If you send invoices weekly and want to see who has paid: the InvoiceSonic tax invoice generator.
  • If most invoices are B2B and over $1,000: ScaleSuite or InvoiceSonic, for the client-ABN field.
  • If you need a deposit invoice before the work starts: send a proforma first. See proforma vs tax invoice.

When not to use InvoiceSonic or a free generator. If you run payroll, lodge BAS from your invoicing data or want your accountant in the same file, use Xero or MYOB. If you schedule jobs and quote on site, Tradify or ServiceM8 combine that with invoicing.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a tax invoice generator ATO-compliant?

It must be able to output all seven details the ATO requires: the words "Tax invoice", the seller's identity, the seller's ABN, the date issued, a description of each item with quantity and price, the GST amount (or a statement that the total includes GST), and the buyer's identity or ABN for sales of $1,000 or more. Without an ABN field or a buyer field, it cannot produce a valid tax invoice for every sale.

Which tax invoice generator is completely free with no signup?

Invoice-Generator.com.au, ScaleSuite, PayCal and Refrens all let you build and download a PDF tax invoice without an account. Xero's generator requires your name and email before download, and Zoho Invoice requires an account.

Do I need a tax invoice generator if I am not registered for GST?

No. If you are not registered for GST you issue a plain invoice, not a tax invoice, and you do not charge GST. Most tools above have a GST toggle that produces a standard invoice with no GST line and no "Tax invoice" heading.

Does a tax invoice generator need a field for my customer's ABN?

Only for sales of $1,000 or more (GST-inclusive), where the ATO requires the buyer's identity or ABN. A customer name and address satisfies the identity requirement, so a dedicated buyer-ABN field is convenient rather than mandatory.
