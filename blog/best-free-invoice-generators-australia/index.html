1. InvoiceSonic — Best overall

InvoiceSonic is free invoicing software that creates, sends and tracks a professional invoice from your phone or browser in under a minute. It suits sole traders, tradies and contractors. What sets it apart from every other free tool here is the after-work: an invoice tracker shows who has paid, and automatic reminders chase the ones who haven't.

It passes all three tests. Your first invoice needs no signup, there is no watermark, and the Australian tax format applies GST correctly (plus NZ, UK, US and Canadian formats across 12 currencies). It charges 0% transaction fee and gets you paid by bank transfer or PayID with no gateway to set up. It is free invoicing software built and supported by its founder, so a real person answers the support email.

Pros

  • Sends, tracks and reminds — the three things a PDF generator cannot do.
  • No signup for the first invoice, no watermark, 0% InvoiceSonic transaction fee.
  • Saves your business name, logo and clients; recurring billing; iPhone app.

Cons

  • You will eventually create an account to keep your invoice history.
  • Invoicing only — no payroll, bank feeds or BAS; some automation sits on the Pro upgrade.

Price: Free tier with a Pro upgrade (see the site for current pricing). Best for: anyone sending more than a handful of invoices a month who wants to know who has paid.

Invoice-Generator.com.au is a 100% free invoice generator that turns a form into a downloadable PDF with no signup, no watermark and no ads. It is built for people who need one correct document right now. What distinguishes it is privacy and compliance: invoice data never leaves your browser, and the Australian tax invoice generator adds an ABN field, 10% GST and a live checklist that ticks off each ATO requirement as you type.

It passes all three tests: no watermark, no signup, and the Australian mode produces a "Tax Invoice" heading with ABN and GST line. Totals calculate as you add lines and you can upload a logo. If you'd rather start from a layout, the tax invoice template and the profession-specific invoice templates (tradie, plumber, electrician, cleaner and more) come pre-filled.

Price: Free, with no paid tier. Best for: a correct Australian tax invoice in two minutes, no strings attached.

3. Zoho Invoice free invoice generator — Best gateway to invoicing software

Zoho's free generator is a form-based builder that sits in front of Zoho Invoice, a genuinely free invoicing app for small businesses. It suits people who expect to outgrow a PDF. The distinguishing feature is the free plan behind it: up to 500 invoices a year, two users and three projects at no cost.

As a pure generator it is weaker. You can add a logo and a tax line, but the page prompts you to sign up to save and send, and custom fields (where an ABN would go) only exist inside the Zoho Invoice account. Watermark policy is not stated, and the stand-alone generator has no "Tax invoice" heading.

Pros

  • The free Zoho Invoice plan is real: 500 invoices a year, reminders, recurring invoices, iOS and Android mobile app.
  • Grows into expenses, timesheets and projects.

Cons

  • Signup pushed at the download step; the generator is a lead magnet.
  • No ABN field or "Tax invoice" heading on the stand-alone generator; watermark policy not stated.

Price: Free (generator and Zoho Invoice free plan); Zoho Books is paid — check current pricing. Best for: small businesses growing from a generator into invoicing software.

4. Wise invoice generator — Best for overseas clients

Wise's generator is a no-signup form that produces a PDF in any of its supported currencies. It suits Australian freelancers who invoice clients in the US, UK or Europe. The distinguishing feature is currency handling: you switch the invoice currency in a click.

No signup is required and Wise states you can create unlimited invoices. The tax field is generic (it talks about VAT), there is no ABN field and the heading is "Invoice", so an Australian tax invoice needs manual edits. Watermark policy is not stated.

Pros

  • No signup, no stated invoice limit, instant PDF download.
  • Invoice currency selectable per document; pairs with a Wise account for foreign payments.

Cons

  • No ABN field and no "Tax invoice" heading.
  • No sending, tracking or reminders; watermark policy not stated.

Price: Free. Best for: invoicing in USD, GBP or EUR to be paid into Wise.

5. Xero free invoice generator — Best if you're about to move to Xero

Xero's "free invoice generator" is an editable PDF template, not a web app: you enter your first name, email and country, and Xero emails you a fillable PDF with a how-to guide. The distinguishing feature is that you get both a tax and a non-tax version, so unregistered sole traders get the right document without a GST line.

It fails the signup test by design — the email form is the product. The tax version is built for Australian GST, so the fields line up with an ATO tax invoice. We could not confirm whether the PDF carries Xero branding. Because it is a fillable PDF, invoice numbers, dates and totals are manual.

Pros

  • Tax and non-tax versions designed for Australian GST.
  • Easy step to Xero proper for bank feeds, BAS and payroll.

Cons

  • Email address required before you get anything.
  • Static PDF with no automatic totals; a funnel to a paid Xero plan.

Price: Template is free; Xero subscriptions are paid — check current AUD pricing. Best for: a Xero-style tax invoice while you decide whether to buy Xero.

6. Square Invoices — Best for card payments on invoices

Square Invoices is a free invoicing app that sends unlimited invoices, estimates and contracts and takes card payments on them. The distinguishing feature is the payment link: the client pays by card from the invoice email, and Square settles to your bank next business day at no extra cost.

A Square account is required, so there is no anonymous download. PDFs are clean with no watermark. The Australian setup supports GST on invoices and prints your business details including ABN, so the result works as a tax invoice. The catch is the fee: 2.2% on online card payments at the time of writing, which is $110 on a $5,000 job. Bank transfer costs nothing.

Pros

  • Free plan with unlimited invoices, estimates and contracts; tracks paid and unpaid.
  • Card payment from the invoice; next-day transfer at no cost; mobile app.

Cons

  • Account required.
  • 2.2% online card fee; Invoices Plus AU$30 a month (at the time of writing).

Price: Free plan; Invoices Plus AU$30/month; 2.2% online card fee (at the time of writing). Best for: businesses that want "pay now" on the invoice.

When not to use InvoiceSonic or a free generator

If you employ staff, you need payroll, super and STP reporting — that is Xero or MYOB territory. If you run a crew and the real problem is scheduling jobs and quoting on site, look at Tradify or ServiceM8, where the invoice falls out of the job card. And if a large customer requires e-invoicing over Peppol, none of the ten tools above sends Peppol e-invoices.

What to look for in a free invoice generator

  • ATO tax invoice fields. A valid tax invoice must show the words "Tax invoice", your identity, your ABN, the invoice date, a description of what was sold, the GST amount, and the buyer's identity for sales of $1,000 or more. A tool without an ABN field makes you improvise. Our guide to tax invoice requirements in Australia covers each one.
  • No watermark. Download a test PDF before you use any tool for real; six of the ten above do not state their policy.
  • No forced signup. Only three tools here download with no account.
  • Automatic calculations and sequential numbers. Line totals, GST and grand total should compute as you type, and the tool should make the invoice number and invoice date easy to set. See how to write an invoice for numbering rules.
  • Saved business details and logo. Retyping your business name, ABN and BSB is how errors creep in; InvoiceSonic, Square and Bookipi save them.
  • Custom fields, and a path to sending. Purchase order numbers and site addresses get invoices approved faster; check whether custom fields are free or gated. And if you send more than a few invoices a month, pick a tool that can email, track and remind.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free invoice generator in Australia?

For a one-off PDF, Invoice-Generator.com.au: no signup, no watermark, and its Australian mode produces a tax invoice with an ABN field, 10% GST and a "Tax Invoice" heading. If you also need to send, track and chase invoices, InvoiceSonic is the better free tool because it does all of that at no cost.

Do free invoice generators add a watermark?

Some do, and most don't say so until you download. InvoiceSonic and Invoice-Generator.com.au both state there is no watermark. None of the eight third-party tools here states a no-watermark guarantee, so download a test PDF and check the footer first.

Is a free invoice generator's PDF a valid tax invoice for the ATO?

Only if the PDF shows the words "Tax invoice", your identity and ABN, the invoice date, a description of what you sold, the GST amount, and the buyer's identity for sales of $1,000 or more. Invoice-Generator.com.au's Australian mode and InvoiceSonic's Australian GST format cover these; several overseas generators do not have an ABN field at all.

Do I need to register for GST before I can issue a tax invoice?

Yes. You can only issue a tax invoice and charge 10% GST once you are registered for GST, which is compulsory once your turnover reaches $75,000 a year. Below that you can still invoice, but the document should be titled "Invoice", not "Tax invoice", and it should not show a GST line.

Which free invoice generator works without signing up?

Invoice-Generator.com.au, Wise and InvoiceSonic (for your first invoice) let you build and download a PDF with no account. Zoho Invoice, Bookipi, Square, Invoice Home and Xero all ask for an email address or an account before you get the file.

Can I use a free invoice generator on my phone?

Yes. InvoiceSonic has an iPhone app and works in a mobile browser; Bookipi, Invoice Simple, Invoice Home and Square have iOS and Android apps. Invoice-Generator.com.au is a responsive web page, so it works on a phone without installing anything.
