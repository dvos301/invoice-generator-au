The $75,000 question: which group are you in?

In Australia you must register for GST once your GST turnover reaches $75,000 in a 12-month period, and you have 21 days to do it. Below that line your invoice carries no GST and you lodge no BAS, so a paid app's accounting features are dead weight. Above it, you need GST tracking and a BAS-ready report.

  • Before you register for GST — a free generator or free app is enough. InvoiceSonic, Invoice-Generator.com.au and Zoho Invoice cover this stage for $0.
  • After you register — pick an app that totals GST and prepares your BAS: Hnry, Rounded Pro, Sole, Thriday or QuickBooks Online.

3. Hnry — Best for hands-off tax

Hnry is an invoicing app and accounting service in one, built for Australian sole traders and contractors. Clients pay into your Hnry account, Hnry sets aside income tax and GST, lodges your BAS and tax return, and passes the rest to you. You pay a percentage of income rather than a subscription.

Pros

  • Unlimited invoices and quotes, payment reminders, expense tracking and a mobile app in the one fee.
  • GST and income tax calculated and paid on your behalf; BAS lodgement included; specialist sole trader accountants on hand.

Cons

  • Every client payment must flow through your Hnry account, so it cannot be used as a standalone invoicing app.
  • At 1% of income the fee grows with you: $120,000 of income costs $1,200 plus GST a year, more than any subscription here.

Price: 1% + GST of every payment into your Hnry account, capped at $1,500 + GST a year, minimum $0.50 + GST on payments under $50 (at the time of writing). Best for: GST-registered contractors who would rather pay a percentage than think about tax.

What to look for in an invoicing app for sole traders

  • ATO tax invoice fields. Once registered for GST, each invoice over $82.50 must show the words "Tax invoice", your identity, your ABN, the date, a description of what was sold and the GST amount; sales of $1,000 or more must also show the buyer's identity or ABN. If an app cannot print an ABN or a GST line, it is not built for Australian businesses.
  • Sends and tracks. Emailing a PDF is fine at five invoices a month. Beyond that you want sent, viewed and paid status in one place.
  • Payment reminders. Automatic reminders are the biggest single time-saver.
  • GST tracking, if registered. The app should total GST collected and paid each quarter so the BAS is a copy job.
  • Works on a phone. Most sole traders invoice from the ute or the kitchen table; a real mobile app beats a desktop dashboard.
  • Honest pricing. Watch for card fees on top of subscriptions, invoice caps on free plans, and introductory prices that jump at renewal.

Which one should you choose?

  • Fewer than 5 invoices a month, not registered for GST → the free sole trader invoice generator. Download the PDF, email it, keep a copy.
  • Invoices every week, want them tracked and chased → InvoiceSonic. Free, no gateway setup, reminders included.
  • Just crossed $75,000 and want tax handled → Hnry if a percentage suits you, Rounded Pro if you prefer a flat fee.
  • Opening a business bank account anyway → Thriday's free plan.
  • Your accountant has asked for "the file" → QuickBooks Online Simple Start, or Sole to stay with an Australian vendor.

When not to use InvoiceSonic or a free generator. If you take card payments in person, Square Invoices sits on top of the reader you already own (free plan, 2.2% on online card payments at the time of writing). If you have hired your first employee, you need payroll and Single Touch Payroll, which means Xero or MYOB. If you are a tradie juggling jobs and staff, Tradify or ServiceM8 do the scheduling and raise the invoice at the end. Frequently asked questions

What is the best free invoicing app for sole traders in Australia?

InvoiceSonic is the best free invoicing app because it sends invoices, tracks who has paid and chases late payers on a free tier. If you only need a PDF, Invoice-Generator.com.au's sole trader invoice generator is free with no signup, no watermark and nothing stored on a server.

Do I need an invoicing app before I register for GST?

No. Until your GST turnover reaches $75,000 you issue plain invoices with no GST line, so a free generator or a free app covers you. GST tracking and BAS-ready reports only matter once you register.

Which invoicing apps track GST for BAS?

Hnry, Rounded (Pro plan), Sole, Thriday and QuickBooks Online all track GST collected and paid and produce BAS-ready figures. InvoiceSonic, Invoice-Generator.com.au and Zoho Invoice put 10% GST on the invoice but do not prepare a BAS.

Can I invoice as a sole trader without an ABN?

You can, but the client may be required to withhold 47% of the payment under the no-ABN withholding rules. Every app in this list has an ABN field, and applying for an ABN is free through the Australian Business Register.

How much does Hnry cost?

Hnry charges 1% plus GST of every payment into your Hnry account, capped at $1,500 plus GST per year, with a minimum of $0.50 plus GST on payments under $50, at the time of writing. That fee includes invoicing, tax and BAS lodgement and accountant support.
