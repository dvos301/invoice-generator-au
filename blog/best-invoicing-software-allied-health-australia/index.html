How we tested: the mixed-funding test. Three questions. Can it produce three differently shaped invoices in one week, for a private client, an NDIS participant and a plan-managed or DVA client, without rebuilding the template? Can you set the tax treatment invoice by invoice, so one carries a GST line and the next none? And will it send to, and chase, a payer who is not the person you treated?

Allied health is the only field on this site where one clinician sends three shapes of invoice in a week. A private client gets an ordinary tax invoice; an NDIS participant's needs their name and NDIS number, the support item number from the NDIS Price Guide or Support Catalogue, the service dates, the hours and your ABN. A plan manager or a department such as DVA wants it addressed to them, not to the person you treated.

The worked number. Take a A$193.99 consult. If that service is taxable, the GST inside it is one eleventh, or A$17.64. If it is GST-free, the line is A$0.00. Most NDIS supports are GST-free where they are a reasonable and necessary support in a participant's plan, so a clinician billing 20 of those a month with a 10% line on by default is out by A$352.80 a month: A$1,058.40 a quarter, A$4,233.60 a year, in the wrong box at BAS time. Whether a given service is taxable or GST-free depends on the service and the funding source, so confirm your position with the ATO or your accountant before setting a default rate; our GST calculator does the arithmetic.

What to look for in invoicing software for allied health

Allied health billing is not high volume, it is high variety. Twenty invoices a month is nothing; twenty invoices in three shapes, with two tax treatments and three payers, is the problem.

  • Somewhere to put NDIS details without a rebuild. Participant name and NDIS number, support item number, service dates, hours and your ABN all have to fit, every time. Our guide to invoicing software for NDIS providers goes deeper.
  • Control of the tax line, invoice by invoice. GST treatment depends on the service and the funding source, and most NDIS supports are GST-free where they are a reasonable and necessary support in a plan. An app that forces one rate onto everything is wrong half the time, and only the ATO or your accountant can say which half.
  • An invoice you can address to someone other than the client. Plan managers, DVA and other third-party payers receive the invoice even though the participant received the service. Confirm each payer's requirements with that payer; they differ, and no app sets them.
  • Automatic reminders. Third-party payers process in batches, and a missed batch means another month. Our guide on chasing overdue invoices covers the rest.
  • A tax invoice that meets the ATO's list. Where you are registered for GST it must carry the words "Tax invoice", your identity and ABN, the date, a description of what was supplied, the GST amount, and the buyer's identity or ABN for sales of $1,000 or more. Full list in our tax invoice requirements guide.
  • Whether you need to register for GST at all. Registration is compulsory at A$75,000 of turnover. What counts towards that figure when part of your work is GST-free is a question for the ATO or your accountant, so read the GST registration threshold, then get advice on your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best invoicing software for allied health in Australia?

InvoiceSonic. It is the only tool here with a dedicated NDIS invoice template alongside ordinary ABN and sole trader tax invoice templates, so a private consult and an NDIS support are billed from one place. Pro adds recurring invoices, automatic reminders and open tracking for payers who go quiet.

Can one invoicing app handle private, NDIS and plan-managed clients?

Yes, if it lets you change the layout and the tax treatment per invoice rather than forcing one template on everything, and lets you address the invoice to a payer who is not the person you treated. InvoiceSonic ships an NDIS invoice template as well as a standard one. A free PDF generator can build those layouts but will not send them.

Do allied health invoices include GST?

It depends on the service and the funding source, and you should confirm your own position with the ATO or your accountant. Most NDIS supports are GST-free where they are a reasonable and necessary support in a participant's plan, so the same clinician can send a GST-free invoice one day and a taxable one the next. Where a service is taxable, the GST inside a A$193.99 fee is one eleventh: A$17.64.

What has to be on an NDIS invoice?

An NDIS invoice needs the participant's name and NDIS number, the support item number from the NDIS Price Guide or Support Catalogue, the service dates, the hours and your ABN. Plan-managed, self-managed and NDIA-managed participants are billed differently, and NDIA-managed claims go through the myplace provider portal via PRODA.

Yes. Invoice-Generator.com.au is free with no signup, no watermark and no invoice limit, and Zoho Invoice and Wave are both free at the time of writing. A free InvoiceSonic account gives you 3 saved invoices, 1 tracked email send and 1 automatic reminder a month.
