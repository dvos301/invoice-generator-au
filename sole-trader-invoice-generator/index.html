The free invoice generator for Australian sole traders

Sole trader, freelancer or contractor? This generator is built for you: your name and ABN up top, optional 10% GST, PayID payment details, and a live ATO tax-invoice checklist — free, no signup.

What sole traders need on an invoice

  • Your name or business name with your ABN directly beneath it
  • "Tax Invoice" if you are GST-registered and making a taxable sale, or "Invoice" if you are not registered
  • A clear, itemised description of your work
  • Your payment details — BSB, account number and PayID

New to invoicing solo? Read how to invoice as a sole trader and do I need an ABN to invoice.

Do sole traders charge GST?

Charge GST on taxable sales if you are registered. Most businesses must register when current or projected GST turnover reaches $75,000; taxi and ride-sourcing have a separate rule. If you are not registered, set GST to zero and title the document "Invoice". See the ATO registration rules and our GST guide.
