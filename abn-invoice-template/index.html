What is an ABN invoice template?

An ABN invoice template is an invoice layout with a place for your 11-digit Australian Business Number, so clients can see who you are and pay you without withholding tax. The one above starts as a plain “Invoice” with no GST, because most people searching for it have an ABN but are not yet registered for GST. Two changes turn it into a tax invoice when you are.

What must an ABN invoice include?

An ABN invoice should show your name, your ABN, the issue date, what you supplied and the price. If you are registered for GST it must also be titled “Tax Invoice” and show the GST. If you are not registered, title it “Invoice” and leave GST off.

The ATO sets the minimum for tax invoices. For a plain invoice there is no legal checklist, but business.gov.au recommends the same core details plus an invoice number and how you want to be paid.

ABN invoice vs tax invoice: what’s the difference?

Every tax invoice carries an ABN, but not every ABN invoice is a tax invoice. A tax invoice is issued by a GST-registered business for a taxable sale and lets a registered buyer claim the GST back. If you have an ABN but are not registered for GST, the ATO says your invoices should not include the words “tax invoice”.

Switch to a tax invoice from the date your GST registration takes effect. You must register within 21 days of your GST turnover reaching the $75,000 threshold, and taxi and ride-sourcing drivers must register regardless of turnover. Once registered, a customer who asks for a tax invoice must get one within 28 days, unless the sale is $82.50 or less including GST. What happens if you invoice without an ABN?

If you supply a business and don’t quote an ABN, that business generally has to withhold 47% of the payment and send it to the ATO. On Example A’s $840 invoice, that means the client pays you $445.20 and withholds $394.80.

The ATO lists exceptions where no-ABN withholding does not apply, including payments of $75 or less (excluding GST), genuine hobbies, supplies of a wholly private or domestic nature, and individuals under 18 paid no more than $350 a week. A payer can also withhold 47% if it has reasonable grounds to believe the ABN you quoted is invalid or belongs to someone else, so check yours is typed correctly. Registering for an ABN is free through the Australian Business Register. More detail: do I need an ABN to invoice?

Where do you find or check an ABN?

Use ABN Lookup at abr.business.gov.au. You can search by ABN, ACN or name, and the record shows whether the ABN is active or cancelled and its GST status.

Look up your own ABN before your first invoice to confirm the GST status you should be invoicing under. It is also worth looking up a new client’s ABN before you put it on a tax invoice of $1,000 or more.

ABN invoice FAQs

Do I need to put my ABN on an invoice?

If you are invoicing a business, yes in practice. Without your ABN on the invoice or another document, the business generally has to withhold 47% of the payment. A GST-registered business must also show its ABN on every tax invoice.

Can I use an ABN invoice template if I am not registered for GST?

Yes. Title the document Invoice, not Tax Invoice, and leave GST at 0%. That is how the template above starts. You only add 10% GST once your GST registration is in place.

Do I need my client’s ABN on my invoice?

For a tax invoice of $1,000 or more, the ATO requires the buyer’s identity or ABN. Below $1,000, or on a plain invoice from a business that is not GST-registered, the client’s name is enough, though adding their ABN does no harm.

Is an ABN invoice the same as a tax invoice?

No. A tax invoice is a specific document for GST-registered businesses making taxable sales. An ABN invoice from a business that is not registered for GST is a standard invoice and must not be called a tax invoice.

How long do I need to keep copies of my invoices?

Most business records must be kept for at least 5 years. Save a PDF of every invoice you send, in printed or electronic form.
